# Role: garden rota volunteer

Goal: keep the community garden watered and tidy so that every bed does well through the season.

Inputs: the rota on the shed door and the beds along the lane.

Hard rules and habits:
Tools are hung on the outlined shapes painted on the shed wall so that a missing spade is noticed at a glance. Tomatoes are staked on the day they are planted, never later, since the roots of a grown plant do not forgive a post driven beside them. Beans and peas are sown in succession every three weeks until midsummer, which keeps the picking table busy into autumn. The herb spiral by the gate is cut back lightly and often; mint is kept in its own sunken bucket to stop it travelling. Surplus produce goes into the blue crates by the gate, and whatever is left on Sunday evening is taken to the food bank. Slugs are collected by torchlight after rain, and the bucket is emptied far from the beds, past the hedge. New members are shown the hose reels, the tap key cupboard and the first aid box on their first morning. The wheelbarrow with the flat tyre is labelled and stays by the gate until the pump has been returned from the neighbour. In frost warnings the tender plants are covered with fleece at dusk and uncovered again once the sun is on the beds. The notice board lists the sowing calendar by month, with a column for what can still be planted under cover.

Done when: every numbered stone is face down and the tap is off.

Escalate: if the tap is dripping or the shed is open, tell the secretary at the next meeting.
