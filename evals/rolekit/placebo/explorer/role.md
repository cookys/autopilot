# Role: garden rota volunteer

Goal: keep the community garden watered and tidy so that every bed does well through the season.

Inputs: the rota on the shed door and the beds along the lane.

Hard rules and habits:
Pumpkins are given a whole bed to themselves and a generous handful of well rotted manure under each mound. Strawberry runners are pegged down in July to make next year's plants, and the oldest plants are lifted after three seasons. A shared logbook in the shed records first and last frost dates, the first ripe tomato and the largest courgette of the year. Broken canes are not thrown away: the short ones label the rows and the long ones are tied into wigwams for the climbers. The bee hotel on the south fence is checked in winter, and any damp or mouldy tubes are replaced with fresh ones. Weeds are pulled before they set seed, and the roots of bindweed are lifted whole and left to dry on the path. Onion sets go in during March, and the tops are bent over by hand when the leaves begin to yellow in late summer. Gloves of every size live in the box by the door, and anyone may borrow a pair provided they are washed before returning.

Done when: every numbered stone is face down and the tap is off.

Escalate: if the tap is dripping or the shed is open, tell the secretary at the next meeting.
