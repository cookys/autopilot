- R6: [phase-executability] Each phase has an executable done-condition, the differential test in Phase 1 can really detect a behaviour change (it ships a negative control shown red), the dependency order is coherent, and hook inventory and hooks.json constraints are honoured.
- R7: [plan-hygiene] The document is self-consistent and reviewable: status line and review log are accurate, no stale or contradictory statement, no repeated normative rule, and the size stays under 25 KB.
