# Rubric — per-turn gate plan, P0 and P1 only (BL-0055), frozen for plan review

- R1: [evidence-fidelity] Every number and verdict in section 0 is internally consistent (counts, denominators, p-values, arm labels) and no sentence claims more than the two t14 experiments on one task and one haiku alias support; the "must not be extrapolated" caveat is honoured and nothing is asserted as fact that section 3 says P1 must still establish.
- R2: [p0-probe-soundness] The P0 questions A, B and C can be answered by a real run or an official documentation URL as written, the procedure is executable by one hand without inventing infrastructure, the completion labels are unambiguous, the negative control is real, and no answer can rest on reading hooks.json alone.
- R3: [p1-preregistration] The P1 metrics, sample, window, decision rules (labels A to D and U) and non-controlled checklist are fixed before outcomes are read; the label regions have no gap or overlap; the batch validity rule and the no-replacement rule cannot be gamed by dropping or rerunning runs; the eight candidates are handled honestly as consistent, excludes or unresolved, with no cause named early.
- R4: [scope-discipline] The plan covers P0 and P1 only: no design of later phases (gate script, rail adapter, budgets, repair flow, rerun, pilot, default) is carried in as authority; the closing questions are adequate for deciding whether and how to plan later phases; the GO or HOLD rule is coherent and does not deadlock or presuppose a later design.
- R5: [repo-fit] Claims about existing repo objects (hooks.json Stop and SubagentStop wiring, run-t14-gate-batch.js, run-orchestration-eval.sh, check-hands-commit.js and check-repair-scope.js call sites, evidence directory layout) are accurate; ADR-0001, scorecard-first and the version hold are respected; the allowed paths for P0 and P1 are bounded and sufficient to do the work.
- R6: [executability-cost] Each step is executable without hidden steps, the cost and wall-time estimate follows from the numbers in section 0, BLOCKED conditions are explicit, and the time window and authentication assumptions are realistic for this host.
- R7: [plan-hygiene] The document is self-consistent and reviewable: no stale or contradictory statement (status line, review history, review log), no repeated normative rule, the review-history summary is accurate and its artifact paths exist or are marked as history-only, and the size stays under 25 KB.
