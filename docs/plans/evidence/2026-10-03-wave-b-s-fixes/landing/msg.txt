fix(dispatch-hetero): reap race in the zombie fixture behind the intermittent watchdog zombie case (wave-B S fixes row 7)

The five zombie fixtures in dispatch-hetero-watchdog-followups.test.sh used a bash
holder that backgrounds a child and then execs sleep. Under CPU load the child exits
before the exec, bash reaps it, and no zombie ever exists ("never became zombie
(state='gone')"). The watchdog (dispatch-hetero.sh) was behaving correctly. The child
now exits only after the holder's comm is sleep, so the zombie is deterministic.
20 contended runs: 4/20 failed before, 0/20 after.
