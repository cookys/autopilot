# Role: garden rota volunteer

Goal: keep the community garden watered and tidy so that every bed does well through the season.

Inputs: the rota on the shed door and the beds along the lane.

Hard rules and habits:
Slugs are collected by torchlight after rain, and the bucket is emptied far from the beds, past the hedge. New members are shown the hose reels, the tap key cupboard and the first aid box on their first morning. The wheelbarrow with the flat tyre is labelled and stays by the gate until the pump has been returned from the neighbour. In frost warnings the tender plants are covered with fleece at dusk and uncovered again once the sun is on the beds. The notice board lists the sowing calendar by month, with a column for what can still be planted under cover. Visitors are welcome at the open afternoon on the first Saturday of each month, and children may water the sunflower row. Paths between the beds are kept to a width a wheelbarrow can pass, and bark chip is topped up each spring. Water butts are scrubbed once a year in March, when the first warm days make it pleasant work for a small team. Seed packets are stored in a biscuit tin with a silica gel sachet, sorted by the month in which they should be sown. The allotment association meets in the village hall on the last Thursday of the month, and minutes are posted on the door.

Done when: every numbered stone is face down and the tap is off.

Escalate: if the tap is dripping or the shed is open, tell the secretary at the next meeting.
