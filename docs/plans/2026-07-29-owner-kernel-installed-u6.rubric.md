substitution and every other Engine sink are rejected.

terminal result from `committed` or `converged`.

scenario-specific, mutation-proven oracles and no `not_applicable` rows.

conservative policy, abort/recovery, exact disclosure, and the fixed Engine sink.

`HOLD` with exact reasons; no metric redefinition, waiver, or elapsed-time fabrication occurs.

perform any external publication or deployment.
